From armchair to toilet: seven things to check

Illustrative drawing. These are things to check, not treatments shown to work in trials.

By day
  • 1. A chair or bed at a height the person can stand up from
  • 5. A toilet that can be seen
  • 2. A walking aid within reach
  • 6. Clothes that are easy to undo
  • 3. A clear, lit route
  • 7. A raised seat or rails
  • 4. A door that opens easily
At night: a light, and a commode or urinal bottle by the bed
