Ask a care home about the night

Questions about a home's plan, not blame for night staff.

1How are night checks planned for each person, and can they be changed?
2How is continence care given to someone who is asleep?
3How are noise and lights kept down? What happens if someone is awake at 3am?
4What time do residents go to bed, and who decides?
