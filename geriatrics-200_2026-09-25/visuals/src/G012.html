Sarcopenia: start with strength

1. Weak grip or slow chair rises: probable sarcopenia. Look for causes. Start strength exercise. Grip below about 27 kg (men), 16 kg (women). Five chair rises take more than 15 seconds.
2. Low muscle quantity on a measure: confirmed
3. Walking 0.8 m/s or slower: severe

Scan later, if it will change the plan.
