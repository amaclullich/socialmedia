Before tramadol in an older person, check four things

Tramadol also raises serotonin and noradrenaline and depends partly on a variable liver enzyme (CYP2D6).

  • Antidepressants: serotonin toxicity risk, and some SSRIs reduce its pain relief
  • Sodium: in the first month after starting, admissions for low sodium about 4.6 vs 1.9 per 10,000 people, tramadol vs codeine
  • Seizure history: tramadol can cause seizures
  • Kidney function: tramadol leaves mainly through the kidneys; check and adjust

Low sodium figures: UK adults of all ages starting treatment for non-cancer pain, observational, wide uncertainty; uncommon
