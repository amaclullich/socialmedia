How a stopped medicine can come back

Without a check
Hospital stops a tablet
Discharge letter arrives
Home medicines chart updated
GP repeat list unchanged
Next monthly order placed from the repeat list
Supply arrives with the stopped tablet
With a named person
Discharge letter arrives
Repeat list and next order updated within days
Supply matches the discharge letter✓
