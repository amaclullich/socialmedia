Timolol eye drops

Ask about eye drops when the pulse is slow

They get into the bloodstream and can act like a beta-blocker tablet.

Drop in the eye
Enters the bloodstream
Can act like a beta-blocker tablet
Case reports: very slow heart rate and heart block that settled when the drops were stopped
One case report: man aged 62, 13 years on timolol, heart rate 29, heart block settled within 48 hours of stopping; a milder block remained and worsened a year later
Pressing the inner corner of the eye for 5 minutes lowered blood levels in a small study of 21 adults. Whether it prevents a slow pulse was not shown.

Case reports show it can happen, not how often.

Alasdair MacLullichWang et al., BMC Pharmacol Toxicol 2019; Muller et al., 2020
