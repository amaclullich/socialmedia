Adults who lack capacity to consent are often left out of research

15 of about 80 UK trials allowed adults who lacked capacity to take part

UK trials completed 2015 to 2018, conditions where impaired capacity is common. Registry search, not comprehensive.

At least 127 of 434 studies in one geriatrics journal explicitly excluded people with cognitive impairment. Very few gave any justification.
