Small detail, worth checking

Dentures: out at night, cleaned every day

In a Japanese study of people aged 85 and over living at home, sleeping in dentures was linked with more admissions to hospital with pneumonia, or deaths from it. A link does not show cause.

  • Out every night
  • Cleaned every day
  • Stored as your dentist advises
  • In hospital: tell the ward staff you wear dentures
  • In hospital: keep them in a denture pot
  • In hospital: check they are not left on a tray
Alasdair MacLullichIinuma et al., J Dent Res 2015; McReynolds et al., J Prosthodont 2023
