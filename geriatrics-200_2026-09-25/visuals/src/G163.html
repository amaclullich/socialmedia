Teaching round: illustration, not a real case

A care worker's observation is clinical information

A care worker notices a resident is eating less than usual.
He tells a senior colleague, using a set structure.
“She's eating less and sleeping more.”
The nurse takes observations and phones the GP with a clear summary.
The decision is recorded, and the care worker hears what was decided.
“The GP is seeing her today. Tell me if anything changes.”

We should give care workers a structured way to report change, and a named person who responds and reports back.

US trial, 85 nursing homes: INTERACT training did not show a clear drop in admissions overall.
