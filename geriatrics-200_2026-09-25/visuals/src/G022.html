Weighing it up
When to decide about a care home

Recovery after a broken hip takes months

First weeks: in hospital, early in recovery
About 4 months: mood, arm function and thinking at their best (average)
Up to 6 months: most gains in walking and daily activities
Close to 1 year: leg function at its best (average)

Shape for illustration, not measured values

Averages from studies. Not everyone regains their earlier function.

Where it is safe, decide after rehabilitation, with you at the centre.
