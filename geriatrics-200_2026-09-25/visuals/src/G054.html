Reading the evidence

37 in 100 had harm from medicines within 8 weeks

1,116 people aged 65 or over leaving five hospitals in southern England, 2013 to 2015.

37 in 100: some harm from medicines
63 in 100: no harm recorded
Mostly side effects: diarrhoea, constipation, falls, bleeding
About 29 in 100 needed NHS care for it, mostly a GP visit
About 8 in 100 were readmitted
For 4 in 10 with harm, the link was rated only 'possible'
Doctors' predictions at discharge of harm needing healthcare showed no clear link with who needed it
Alasdair MacLullichParekh et al., Br J Clin Pharmacol 2018 (two papers)
