What caused 227 filmed falls in care homes

130 residents, two care homes, British Columbia, 2007 to 2010

Cameras in lounges, dining rooms and corridors only, not bedrooms or bathrooms. Causes judged from video by a research team. Categories shown cover 221 of 227 falls.

Alasdair MacLullichRobinovitch et al., Lancet 2013
