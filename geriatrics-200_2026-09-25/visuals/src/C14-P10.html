Wax can block hearing. Access to removal varies.

  • 1 Small care home studies: wax needing removal in more than half of residents
  • 2 US ENT guideline 2017: check the ears of hearing aid users for wax
  • 3 England, 2020 GP article: practices not obliged to provide removal unless it is commissioned locally
  • 4 Ask your practice where NHS wax removal is provided locally
