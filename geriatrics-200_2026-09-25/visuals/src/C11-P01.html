Sleeping tablets over 60: the average gain

Short trials, as reported by the people taking them.

Average gain

More common with the tablets
Memory problems and confusion
Daytime tiredness
Alasdair MacLullichGlass et al., BMJ 2005: 24 trials, 2,417 people aged 60 and over, 5 nights to 9 weeks
