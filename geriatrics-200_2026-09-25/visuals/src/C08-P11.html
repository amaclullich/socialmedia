Mild to moderate dementia

Is there a cognitive stimulation group near me?

NICE in England says it should be offered. Ask the memory service or GP.

  • Small group, usually twice a week for 7 weeks
  • Talk, word games, puzzles and music
  • Small, short-term boost to memory and thinking; more communication
Alasdair MacLullichNICE NG97; Woods et al., Cochrane 2023
