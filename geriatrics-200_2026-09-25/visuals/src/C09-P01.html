Treatment decisions in medical patients

An MMSE score is not a capacity assessment

Then assess the decision.
Below 20: incapacity more likely (likelihood ratio 6.3)
20 to 24: no clear change (0.87)
Above 24: incapacity less likely, not ruled out (0.14)

MMSE bands only. Not for other tests.

Alasdair MacLullichSessums et al, JAMA 2011
