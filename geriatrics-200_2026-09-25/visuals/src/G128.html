Small detail, worth checking

Could they open it?

Illustrative drawing of a hospital tray. Figures are from Australian studies.

1. Cheese portion: about 4 in 10 healthy people over 65 could not open one

2. Water bottle: 1 in 10 could not

3. Milk, juice and cereal packs: about half of 140 mostly older patients found them difficult

4. In a lab test of 34 well older adults, opening a pack lying in bed took more finger strength than sitting in a chair

Alasdair MacLullichBell et al., Appetite 2013, 2016; J Hum Nutr Diet 2017
