Reading the evidence
Vertebroplasty for spinal fractures

Cement v sham: pain at one month

Average pain score, 0 to 10, five sham-controlled trials, 541 people. The gap is smaller than patients notice.

Sham: made to look like the real procedure, without the cement.

One trial in fractures under 6 weeks old found benefit. At two weeks, 44 in 100 had low pain after cement, against 21 in 100 after sham.

Alasdair MacLullichBuchbinder et al., Cochrane 2018
