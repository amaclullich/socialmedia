Noisy breathing near death: what you may hear

  • Saliva and mucus collect in the throat when a person can no longer clear them
  • Changing the person's position is one usual way to manage the sound
  • Ask the nurse to explain what you are hearing

Studies generally find the sound does not distress the person; few looked at patients
