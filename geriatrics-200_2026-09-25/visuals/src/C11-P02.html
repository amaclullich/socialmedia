Acting out dreams in later life: four steps

A report is a reason to assess, not a diagnosis.

1
Askthe bed partner as well as the person. Dreams of chasing or attacking? Injuries?
2
Assessrefer for a sleep study if in doubt. Severe sleep apnoea can look similar.
3
Protectclear hazards, pad sharp furniture and the headboard, soft rug by the bed.
4
Talkreview medicines, and discuss what it may mean, with time and support.
