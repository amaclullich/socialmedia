Starting hormone treatment for cancer? Ask about your bones

Both treatments speed bone loss and are linked with more fractures.

Prostate cancer
Hormone treatment that lowers testosterone
Breast cancer
Aromatase inhibitors, which remove almost all oestrogen (anastrozole, letrozole, exemestane)

The question to ask

'Can my fracture risk be checked now, at the start?'

Men with prostate cancer who lived 5+ years: 19 in 100 with hormone treatment v 13 in 100 without had broken a bone (link, not proof).

Alasdair MacLullichShahinian et al., NEJM 2005; NOGG 2022
