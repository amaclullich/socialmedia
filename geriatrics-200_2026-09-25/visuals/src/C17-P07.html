Teaching round2/2
When starting or reviewing medicines

Ask: has anything changed with sex or intimacy since you started this medicine?

Many widely used antidepressants, such as sertraline, citalopram and venlafaxine, cause sexual side effects that people mention only if asked. These data are not specific to older adults. In one trial of blood pressure tablets in men aged 45 to 69, new erection problems over 2 years were more common with chlorthalidone (17 in 100) than with placebo (8 in 100); the other four tablets were not clearly different from placebo.

Alasdair MacLullichSerretti and Chiesa, 2009; Grimm et al., Hypertension, 1997
