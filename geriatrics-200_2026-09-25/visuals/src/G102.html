Weighing it up
Illustrative example, not real people

Quinine for night cramps: time for a review?

Man: 'I've taken quinine for my cramps for ages. Is it still doing anything?'
Pharmacist: 'In trials, cramps fell from about 9 to 6 or 7 a fortnight, on low-quality evidence. Rare harms can be serious.'
Pharmacist: 'UK prescribing advice has been to pause it every 3 months to check. Could any of your other medicines be linked?'
Man: 'So we pause it, and I try calf stretches before bed.'

Under the last panel: no harms were reported in small stretching trials; evidence for benefit is mixed
