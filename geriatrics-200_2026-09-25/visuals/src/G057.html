Parkinson's doses: their times, not the ward's

  • Can doses be given at their own times?
  • Can they keep taking their own supply?
  • If they can't swallow or are told not to eat, what is the plan?
Standard used in one English hospital audit: within 30 minutes of the person's own time
Dublin hospital study: 52 in 100 doses more than 30 minutes late
