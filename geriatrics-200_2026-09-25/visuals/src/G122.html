Teaching round

Three facts should travel with every catheter

Hospital ward
Discharge letter
Home or care home
District nurse, GP or continence service

Written down at discharge

  1. Why it went in
  2. The date it went in
  3. Who will review it or take it out, or try without it, and when

UK district nursing survey, 2017 to 2018: half of the people whose catheter had been placed in the previous 4 weeks had a plan to remove it (134 of 267).
