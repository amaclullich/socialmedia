Reading the evidence
Prehabilitation in frail older people

What to say

Can say

May help fitness and recovery

  • Moderate certainty, in adults with cancer
Should not promise

Fewer complications

  • Low or very low certainty
  • Frail trials unclear

Carli 2020: 110 frail patients, programme before vs the same programme after bowel cancer surgery: no clear difference in 30-day complications. About 1 in 7 kept to the after-surgery programme.

Alasdair MacLullichCarli et al., JAMA Surg 2020; McIsaac et al., Br J Anaesth 2022
