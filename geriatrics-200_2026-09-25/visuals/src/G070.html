Before a major operation, ask about daily life

  1. What will a normal day look like at three months?
  2. What is the best case, the worst case and the most likely?
  3. What are the alternatives, and what would each mean for daily life?

US study, 372 people aged 60+, planned major abdominal surgery: washing and dressing took about 3 months to recover on average; household tasks about 6. At 6 months, about 1 in 10 had not recovered basic daily activities.

Alasdair MacLullichLawrence et al., J Am Coll Surg 2004
