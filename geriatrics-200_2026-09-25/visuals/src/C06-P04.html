Small detail, real effect

Three checks for a strong pain patch

  1. 1. Old patch off before the new one goes on. Note where and when.
  2. 2. No heat over the patch: hot water bottles, heat pads, electric blankets.
  3. 3. Fold used patches sticky sides together; dispose of as the pharmacist advises.
A used fentanyl patch still holds enough medicine to harm a child or a pet.
Unusual sleepiness or slow, shallow breathing: get urgent help.
