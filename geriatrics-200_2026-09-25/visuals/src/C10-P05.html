Reading the evidence

Pregabalin for sciatica: no clear benefit, more side effects

Trial of 207 adults, average age early to mid 50s; reduced kidney function excluded.

Leg pain at 8 weeks (0 to 10): pregabalin 3.7, placebo 3.1, no clear difference

People with at least one side effect: pregabalin 64 in 100 (68 of 106), placebo 43 in 100 (43 of 101)

Alasdair MacLullichMathieson et al., NEJM 2017 (PRECISE)
