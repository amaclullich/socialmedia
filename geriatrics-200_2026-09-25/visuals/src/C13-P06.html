Weighing it up

Feeding tubes in advanced dementia

What studies found about tubes
  • No randomised trials
  • PEG tubes not shown to help people live longer (low certainty, observational studies)
  • No study showed more comfort; quality of life never measured
  • More pressure sores with PEG in one study of 4,421 people
Careful hand feeding: what it involves (expert-proposed, not tested)
  • Offer food regularly
  • Stop at coughing, choking or distress
  • Note which foods, helpers and times work
  • Mouth care, talking and touch when eating becomes too hard
  • It is unlikely to stop weight loss

Saying no to a tube is a decision about one treatment. Food, drink, mouth care and company continue.

Alasdair MacLullichDavies et al., Cochrane 2021; AGS 2014; ESPEN 2024
