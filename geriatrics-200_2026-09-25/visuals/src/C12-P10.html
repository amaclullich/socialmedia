Weighing it up2/2
Trial of 75 hospital men, average age about 73

More men said it was comfortable. Combined measure mostly bacteria in urine tests.

Men with a sheath were more likely to say it was comfortable and not painful. The combined measure was mostly bacteria in the urine on daily testing, and was lower with the sheath only after adjustment. In men with dementia the trial did not show a clear difference.

Checklist for a sheath

  • Right size
  • Fitted securely, not tightly
  • Changed daily
  • Skin checked regularly
  • Only for men who can pass urine on their own
