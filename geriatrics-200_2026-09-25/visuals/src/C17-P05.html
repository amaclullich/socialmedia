Small detail, real effect
Risk factors from two heatwave studies

Heatwave: who to check on

  • 1. Top floor under the roof: higher risk
  • 2. Lives alone: higher risk (Chicago)
  • 3. Does not leave home each day, or confined to bed: higher risk
  • 4. Working air conditioning: lower risk (Chicago)
  • 5. Friends nearby and a way to get out: lower risk (Chicago)
  • 6. Light clothing and ways to cool down: lower risk (France)

Check daily on older neighbours who live alone. This is a judgement drawn from studies of who died; they show links, not proof of cause.

Alasdair MacLullichSemenza et al., NEJM, 1996 (Chicago 1995); Vandentorren et al., Eur J Public Health, 2006 (France 2003)
