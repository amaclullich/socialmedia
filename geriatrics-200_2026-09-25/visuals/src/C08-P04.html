Reading the evidence
DAPA trial, 494 people with mild to moderate dementia

Fitness rose in class (exercise group only); memory decline not slowed

  • 1Fitness: walked about 18 metres further in 6 minutes during classes (measured in the exercise group only)
  • 2Memory and thinking at 12 months: not slowed; slightly worse, importance uncertain
  • 3Falls, daily activities, quality of life: no clear difference
Alasdair MacLullichLamb et al., BMJ 2018
