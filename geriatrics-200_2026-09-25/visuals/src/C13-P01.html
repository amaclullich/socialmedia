What came after

A year later: strongly contrasting blue also worked; pale red and pale blue did not
Later study, blue plates, 18 people with dementia, many pale foods on the menu: no clear increase in eating
2026 review: 4 studies, 64 people, none randomised, longest 30 days. Evidence limited, low quality, inconclusive

Cheap to try. Keep helping at the meal.
