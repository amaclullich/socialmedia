Weighing it up
Two trials, two different questions

Breathlessness: our suggestion is a fan first, and oxygen only if it helps

Fan trial: 50 people with advanced illness, short-term
  • Fan on the face for 5 minutes eased breathlessness more than the fan on the legs
Oxygen trial: 239 people whose blood oxygen was not low, 7 days
  • Oxygen eased breathlessness no more than room air
  • Morning breathlessness fell slightly in both groups
Change in morning breathlessness (0 to 10): oxygen -0.9, room air -0.7

Oxygen trial: people with low blood oxygen were not included.

Alasdair MacLullichGalbraith, J Pain Symptom Manage 2010; Abernethy, Lancet 2010
