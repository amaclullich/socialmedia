Weighing it up

A low HbA1c on insulin or gliclazide: ask two questions

  1. Have there been any low blood sugars?
  2. Is this dose still needed?
Schematic, not measured data

HbA1c shows average blood sugar. It does not show low sugars.

Norfolk: about 3 in 10 people aged 70+ with type 2 diabetes on these drugs had HbA1c below 53 mmol/mol
