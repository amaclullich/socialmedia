Small detail, real effect

Three checks for your walking stick

  • 1. Height: top of stick level with the wrist crease
  • 2. Hand: opposite the painful or weak leg
  • 3. Tip: worn smooth? Replace it

44 in 100 sticks were the wrong length (50 users, one English clinic). Both legs weak or poor balance? Ask about a frame.
