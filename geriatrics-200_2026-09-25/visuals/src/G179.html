A one-page plan for if you are suddenly ill

Practical advice, not a research finding

Six things to put on the page

  • Their name and main needs
  • Their medicines, and where they are kept
  • Who to call first, and who second
  • Keys, and how to get in
  • Their daily routine
  • Their GP and pharmacy

Give a copy to each person named on it. Ask your council or a local carers' centre whether they can help you write one.
