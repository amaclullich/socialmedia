Social isolation: still linked

Loneliness: not clearly linked

In this one study, social isolation was still linked with death and loneliness was not clearly linked. Larger reviews still link loneliness with a higher risk of dying, less strongly than isolation. We should still act on loneliness, because it is painful to live with.

Alasdair MacLullichSteptoe et al., PNAS, 2013 (observational, about 7 years of follow-up)
