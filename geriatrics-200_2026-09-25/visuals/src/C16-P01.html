Before a medicine goes into food or drink: five checks

Suggested order, professional judgement. Check the law and guidance where you work.

1Is each medicine still needed?
2Capacity for this decision assessed and written down
3Best-interests decision recorded, with prescriber and family
4Pharmacist advice on how each medicine can be given
5Plan in the care record, with a review date

Norwegian nursing homes, older data: in one week, medicines were hidden in food or drink for 1 in 9 residents of regular units and 1 in 6 in dementia units; this was written in the record in 4 in 10 of those cases

Alasdair MacLullichKirkevold and Engedal, BMJ 2005; Tan et al, BMJ Open 2022
