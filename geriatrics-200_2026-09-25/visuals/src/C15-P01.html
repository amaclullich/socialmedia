37.5°C scores 0 on NEWS2. For this person it is a 1.5°C rise.

Illustrative example: usual temperature 36.0°C
Fever rule: more than 1.1°C above usual

US care home guidance counts a rise of more than 1.1°C above usual as a fever.

  • Compare the reading with the person's usual temperature.
  • A low score should never override a professional's or carer's concern.
