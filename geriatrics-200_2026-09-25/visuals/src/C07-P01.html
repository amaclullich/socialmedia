Switching off ICD shocks: a conversation to have early

  1. No operation: the device is reprogrammed from outside the body
  2. Painless, and it can be reversed
  3. Pacing keeps working
  4. A do-not-resuscitate decision does not switch shocks off
Alasdair MacLullichWan & Chakrabarti, CMAJ 2021; BHRS guideline, Age Ageing 2024
