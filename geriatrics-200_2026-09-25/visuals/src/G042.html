Illustrative example

Two questions about the same stairs

Clinician: "Any difficulty with the stairs?" Patient: "No, I manage."
At home, she climbs one step at a time, both hands on the rail.
Clinician: "Have you changed how you do it, or how often?" Patient: "I only go up once a day now, and slowly."

Changing how you do a task, before any difficulty, was linked with later difficulty in more able women in their 70s in Baltimore (1990s studies).

Alasdair MacLullichFried et al. 2000, 2001; Weiss et al. 2012
