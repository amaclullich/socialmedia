Charles Bonnet syndrome

Seeing things that are not there after sight loss? Tell your doctor.

Vivid images that are not there, seen by people who usually know they are not real.

Illustration
  • Linked with sight loss, most often from macular degeneration
  • Mention new images to a doctor so other causes can be ruled out
  • Explaining the condition is a large part of the help
