Sudden hearing loss in one ear needs checking urgently

One ear sounds muffled on waking.
Our practical advice, not guideline wording: ask to be seen urgently and say: my hearing has dropped suddenly in one ear.
A clinician checks whether the ear canal is blocked, or the inner ear or nerve is affected.
Steroids are an option if started within about 2 weeks. A Cochrane review of 3 small trials found their value unclear.
Alasdair MacLullichAAO-HNS guideline 2019; Wei et al., Cochrane 2013
