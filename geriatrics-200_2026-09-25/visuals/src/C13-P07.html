Single bedside signs miss dehydration

Low-intake dehydration, people aged 65 and over

Not useful alone
  • Urine colour
  • Urine specific gravity
  • Dry mouth
  • Thirst
  • Heart rate
  • Recorded fluid intake
Reference test, screen, and a sign to watch
  • Serum osmolality (295 mOsm/kg or more: starting or present)
  • Calculated osmolarity as a screen
  • Missed drinks between meals: promising in one study of 71
Alasdair MacLullichHooper et al., Cochrane 2015
Teaching round2/2

Estimate osmolarity from routine bloods

A screen, not a diagnosis. Best of 39 formulas in 595 older people.

1.86 x (sodium + potassium) + 1.15 x glucose + urea + 14

All in mmol/L. Result in mOsm/L.

At 295 mOsm/L or more: picks up about 85 in 100 dehydrated people; flags about 41 in 100 who are not
