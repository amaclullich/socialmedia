What gets counted

Score the pain when moving, too

After surgery, pain on movement was about 2 to 3 times as high as pain at rest in the first 3 days, in trials that measured both. The trials were not specifically in older people.

  • 1Pain at rest: ask while lying or sitting
  • 2Pain on standing, turning or walking: ask during the movement
  • 3Can't describe pain? Watch the face and body during movement or care
  • 4Ask: what is stopping you from doing what you want to do?
Alasdair MacLullichSrikandarajah and Gilron, Pain 2011; Schofield, Age Ageing 2018
