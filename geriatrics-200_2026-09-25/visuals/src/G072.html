Teaching round

A time-limited trial, from agreement to review

Core items from the ATS 2024 consensus for intensive care (16 elements in full)

  1. Agree: offered to the person or family, who can agree to it
  2. Name: the treatment and how long it will run
  3. Define: the signs that would show it is working or not
  4. Review on the agreed date: carry on, stop and focus on comfort, or extend
Alasdair MacLullichKruser et al., Ann Am Thorac Soc 2024
