What to look for

Constipation: hard or lumpy stools, straining, a feeling of not having emptied, or fewer than 3 a week, a good part of the time.

  1. Tell your GP about a change from your usual pattern that lasts
  2. Tell your GP about blood from the back passage or in the stool
  3. Tell your GP about weight loss you can't explain
