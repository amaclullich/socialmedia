Ask. Record. Review.

Ask where the person would like to be cared for.
Where would you like to be cared for if you become more unwell?
Write down the answer and the reasons.
Ask again as things change.
Is this still what you want?

In 61 UK studies, many people's preferences were not asked, expressed or recorded. In a separate review, about 1 in 5 patients (10 studies) changed their preference as illness progressed; about 4 in 5 did not.
