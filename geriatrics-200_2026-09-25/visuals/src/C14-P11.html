EULAR recommendations

Active giant cell arteritis: start high-dose steroid immediately

High-dose steroid at once: 40 to 60 mg a day of prednisone or equivalent
Confirm the diagnosis by scan or biopsy
Authors of a Danish study advise prompt ultrasound, preferably within 3 days of starting steroid
Sight loss can be permanent
Our view, not EULAR wording: we should not hold treatment for a scan slot.
Alasdair MacLullichHellmich et al., EULAR 2018 update, Ann Rheum Dis 2020; Hansen et al., Rheumatology 2025

Sensitivity of temporal artery ultrasound

Temporal artery ultrasound picked up less after 10 days of steroids

Before steroids: 73 in 100
After 10 days of steroids: 53 in 100

48 people with newly diagnosed giant cell arteritis, Denmark. 95% confidence intervals 58 to 85 and 38 to 68. Small single-centre study.

Alasdair MacLullichHansen et al., Rheumatology 2025
