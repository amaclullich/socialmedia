Reading the evidence

The higher death rate was seen only in carers who reported strain

US spouse carers aged 66 to 96, followed for about 4 years

Adjusted relative risk of death over about 4 years. 1.0 means the same as non-carers.

Later population studies: carers as a whole lived as long as, or longer than, non-carers. All findings are observational.

Alasdair MacLullichSchulz and Beach, JAMA, 1999; Roth et al., Gerontologist, 2015
