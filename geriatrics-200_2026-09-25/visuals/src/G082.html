Care that works
WHELD trial, 847 residents, 9 months

A care programme tested in 69 nursing homes

  • 1Staff trained in person-centred care
  • 2Social activity tailored to each resident: aiming for an hour a week
  • 3GP prompted to review antipsychotics
  • 4Two champions per home
What changed
  • Quality of life: slightly better
  • Agitation: slightly less
  • Antipsychotic use: no change (under 1 in 10 at start)
  • Rated by care staff; effects small
Alasdair MacLullichBallard et al., PLoS Med 2018
