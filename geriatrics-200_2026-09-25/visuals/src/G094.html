A judgement, with one trial behind it

Talk it through in a structured way, and write it down

In a US trial of 313 people with heart or kidney disease and the person chosen to speak for them, a structured conversation with a trained professional, ending in a written record of goals, left the person chosen understanding those goals much better than usual care.

Alasdair MacLullichKirchhoff et al, J Am Geriatr Soc 2010
