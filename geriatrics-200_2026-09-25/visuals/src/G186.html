Parkinson's and swallowing: decide, then check the chart

1Can no longer swallow
2Ask what is causing discomfort. Stopping Parkinson's medicines suddenly can bring severe stiffness, pain and anxiety. Decide on purpose.
3If a non-oral route is chosen: a rotigotine patch is the most widely used. No trials at the end of life. Too high a dose is linked with confusion and agitation.
4Check the chart: a 2025 review lists metoclopramide and haloperidol as contraindicated in Parkinson's (in its section on surgery)

Doses are approximate. Agree them with pharmacy or the Parkinson's team.
