Care that works
Illustrative example of the kind of visit tested

Working on the person and the home together

US trial, 300 older people on low incomes: the half who had the visits reported less difficulty with basic daily tasks such as washing and dressing at 5 months; no longer clear at 12 months.

  1. Occupational therapist: up to 6 visits, working on a goal the person chose
  2. Nurse: up to 4 visits
  3. Handyperson: repairs and equipment, such as steadying the stairs
  4. The person's own goal sets the plan
Alasdair MacLullichSzanton et al., JAMA Intern Med 2019
