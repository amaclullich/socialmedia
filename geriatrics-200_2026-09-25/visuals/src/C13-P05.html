Comfort at the bedside

Easing a dry mouth in the last days of life

Illustration, not a real person
  • Sips of drink, if they want them
  • Frequent mouth and lip care
  • Ice chips or moistened lips
  • Family can help, if they wish
  • Ask the nurse to show you how to do it safely

Fluids by drip or under the skin are a separate decision, made for each person.

Alasdair MacLullichNICE NG31, 2015; McCann et al., JAMA 1994 (32 people, cancer)
