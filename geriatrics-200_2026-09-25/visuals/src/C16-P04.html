Reading the evidence

Of 100 care home residents in England and Wales in 2009, about 74 were alive a year later

About 26 died within the year. A group figure doesn't predict one person. Ask the doctor: what do you expect for this person?

England and Wales, 2009. Residents aged 65 to 104 already living in a care home (9,772 people), followed for one year.

Alasdair MacLullichShah et al, Age and Ageing 2013
