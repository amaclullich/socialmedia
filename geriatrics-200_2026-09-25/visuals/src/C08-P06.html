Dementia with Lewy bodies: core features

Before an antipsychotic: four questions

Does alertness or thinking vary a lot?
Does the person see people or animals that are not there?
Do they act out dreams in sleep?
Are they slow, stiff or tremulous?
Any yes: think of Lewy body disease and get specialist advice first. A teaching prompt, not a diagnosis.
