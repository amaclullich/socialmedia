Small detail, real effect
About 1 in 3 older people had trouble swallowing (one Australian unit)

Watch the first drink after hip surgery

Sit fully upright, and stay upright for at least 30 minutes after a meal
1. Coughing or choking
2. Hoarse or changed voice after swallowing
3. Several swallows for one mouthful
4. Food left in the mouth

Many people don't notice these themselves

If you see these signs, ask for a swallowing assessment.

Alasdair MacLullichLove et al, Age Ageing 2013; Baijens et al, Clin Interv Aging 2016
