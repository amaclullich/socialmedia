Restless legs: check iron and medicines first

2025 US guideline (AASM). Iron cut-offs are expert consensus, not tested directly.

Step 1Check ferritin and transferrin saturation
Step 2Offer iron if ferritin is 75 or below, or transferrin saturation is under 20%
Step 3Look for aggravators: alcohol, caffeine, sedating antihistamines, serotonergic antidepressants, dopamine blockers, untreated sleep apnoea
Step 4Choose treatment with care
Why the guideline advises against dopamine agonists as standard treatment: augmentation (conditional recommendation)
Gabapentinoids in older people: sleepiness and dizziness, weigh after a fall
Alasdair MacLullichWinkelman et al., J Clin Sleep Med 2025; AGS Beers Criteria 2023
