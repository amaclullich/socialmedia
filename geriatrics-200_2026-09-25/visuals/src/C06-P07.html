Medicines that are not in the boxes at home

  • Injections every few months, such as a 6-monthly bone injection (denosumab)
  • Anything given or supplied by a hospital clinic
  • Anything you buy: vitamins, supplements, herbal remedies
  • Eye drops
  • Inhalers
  • Patches
  • Creams
About 1 in 3 older people on prescriptions in one small English survey (39% replied) also took a supplement or herbal remedy.
Alasdair MacLullichTam et al., CMAJ 2005; Agbabiaka et al., Br J Gen Pract 2018
