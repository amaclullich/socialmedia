Before a tablet is crushed

1. Ask how the tablets go down. About 1 in 7 older people at home say they find it hard (small review).
2. Crushing can change how a medicine works.
3. Slow-release tablets (often marked MR, SR or XL): don't crush or split.
4. Ask a pharmacist first: there may be a liquid, a tablet that dissolves in water, or another medicine.
Hiding medicine in food without the person knowing needs a formal decision about their capacity and care (the legal route differs across the UK).
Alasdair MacLullich
