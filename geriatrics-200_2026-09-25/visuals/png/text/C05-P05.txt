Clinical Frailty Scale (CFS), in plain words
What the frailty number in your letter means

Scored on your usual self, such as two weeks before the illness

1 to 3: fit or managing well
4: slowed down, no daily help needed
5: help with money, transport, heavy housework
6: help outside the home, housework, bathing
7: help with all personal care
8: as 7, and near the end of life
9: expected to live under about 6 months, not otherwise severely frail
Ask: what was it based on?
Alasdair MacLullich
Rockwood and Theou, Can Geriatr J 2020
