READING THE EVIDENCE
Out of every 100 people given CPR: how many survived to leave hospital
CPR on television and in UK hospitals
 TV

US TV programmes, 1990s: 67 in 100 appeared to survive

UK hospitals

UK hospitals, audit data: 18 in 100 survived

Survived to leave hospital
Did not

For the most common kind of heart stop in UK hospitals: about 1 in 10

The two figures are not like for like. UK figures cover adults of all ages and vary by type of arrest and by hospital. TV patients were mostly young people with injuries. Not a prediction for any one person.

Alasdair MacLullich
