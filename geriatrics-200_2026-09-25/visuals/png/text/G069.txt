WEIGHING IT UP
Weighing it up
Kidney failure in later life: two options to weigh

NICE (England): offer both, and decide together with the person

Dialysis

Regular sessions to clean the blood

Conservative kidney care

Active care without dialysis: treating symptoms, protecting remaining kidney function, planning ahead

Older US veterans (observational study): compared with usual medical care, starting dialysis early was linked with little clear difference in survival over 3 years and about 2 weeks fewer days at home

Ask about each: time at home, travel, hospital visits, symptoms
