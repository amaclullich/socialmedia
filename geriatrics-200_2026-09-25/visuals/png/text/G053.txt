READING THE EVIDENCE
Where did the effect go?
WITHIN THE REVIEWED GROUP
Found: 86 in 100 reviewed had a recommendation
Acted on: about 6 in 10 of those had at least one change by 2 months
COMPARED WITH CONTROL
On an overused medicine at 2 months: about 42 in 100 in each group
Readmitted because of a medicine within a year: about 22 in 100 in each group
NO CLEAR DIFFERENCE
Did it change what patients value? Readmission for a medicine: no clear difference. Quality of life: about 2 points higher on a 100-point scale.
Alasdair MacLullich
Blum et al. (OPERAM), BMJ 2021
