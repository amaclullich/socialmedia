TEACHING ROUND
Leg pain on walking: spine or arteries?
Points to the spine (lumbar spinal stenosis)
No pain when sitting
Eases on bending forward, as on a shopping trolley
Points to the arteries
Calf pain eases on standing still
Bending forward does not help
No single answer settles it, and the two can occur together: check foot pulses or ankle pressure anyway.
Alasdair MacLullich
Suri et al., JAMA 2010; Nadeau et al., Can J Surg 2013; Uesugi et al., J Orthop Sci 2012
