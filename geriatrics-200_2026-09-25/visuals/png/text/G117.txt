Women who keep getting urine infections: 24 in 100 without cranberry, 18 in 100 with it. Probably fewer infections (moderate certainty).
Older people in care homes or other institutions: about 11 in 100 either way. No clear benefit shown. Uncertain: anything from about 4 fewer to about 3 more per 100.
Alasdair MacLullich
