WEIGHING IT UP
A year after knee replacement

About 13 in 100 still had troublesome knee pain. At 3 months it was about 22 in 100. The true figures are probably higher.

Troublesome knee pain at 1 year: about 13 in 100
Other 87 in 100: did not report troublesome pain in these studies

Ask: what are my chances of still having pain afterwards?

Ask: what else could I try first?

Alasdair MacLullich
Cheng et al., BMJ Open 2025 (68 studies, nearly 600,000 people)
