TEACHING ROUND
Expert review and observational evidence; no trials
Parkinson's and swallowing: decide, then check the chart
1
Can no longer swallow
2
Ask what is causing discomfort. Stopping Parkinson's medicines suddenly can bring severe stiffness, pain and anxiety. Decide on purpose.
3
If a non-oral route is chosen: a rotigotine patch is the most widely used. No trials at the end of life. Too high a dose is linked with confusion and agitation.
4
Check the chart: a 2025 review lists metoclopramide and haloperidol as contraindicated in Parkinson's (in its section on surgery)

Doses are approximate. Agree them with pharmacy or the Parkinson's team.

Alasdair MacLullich
Beckers et al., J Parkinsons Dis 2025; Hewer et al., 2024
