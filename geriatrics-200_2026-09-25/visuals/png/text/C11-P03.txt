CARE THAT WORKS
2/2
Read it with care
29 of 77 people who tried to taper in a second Quebec trial reported withdrawal symptoms; none needed hospital care
Both trials ran through community pharmacies in Quebec
Mailing information did not reduce use of high-risk medicines (including sedatives) in a large US trial in people with dementia
Ask your pharmacist for written information on the risks and a slow, agreed plan for cutting down.
Alasdair MacLullich
Martin et al., JAMA 2018; Singh et al., JAMA Intern Med 2024
