SMALL DETAIL, REAL EFFECT
Write each fall down on the day

In a US study, 13 to 32 in 100 people with a confirmed fall did not recall falling in the months they were asked about.

TEMPLATE
Date and time
Where
What you were doing
How you felt just before
Could you get up?
How long on the floor?

Take this to every appointment. An hour or more on the floor is a sign of high risk: tell the doctor.

Alasdair MacLullich
Cummings et al., J Am Geriatr Soc 1988; World Falls Guidelines 2022
