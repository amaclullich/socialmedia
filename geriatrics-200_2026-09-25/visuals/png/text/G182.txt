ASK THIS
US study: 323 care home residents with advanced dementia, followed for 18 months
Ask: what will the later stages of dementia bring?
Eating problem: about 86 in 100
Fever: about 53 in 100
Pneumonia: about 41 in 100
Died during the study: about 55 in 100

Figures are for late-stage dementia in US care homes only.

Three questions to ask the care team
What usually happens in the later stages?
Would going to hospital help?
What will be done for comfort either way?
Alasdair MacLullich
Mitchell et al., N Engl J Med 2009
