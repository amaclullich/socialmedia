CARE THAT WORKS
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
Going with a parent to an appointment
She has been dizzy in the mornings.
Without a plan, the daughter answers for her mother.
I will tell her about the mornings.
And I will add anything you miss.
The evening before, they agree who says what.
What do you think, Mum?
In the room, the daughter asks what her mother thinks.

Agree beforehand who says what. Ask questions that let them speak.

Alasdair MacLullich
Clayman et al, 2005; Laidsaar-Powell et al, 2013
