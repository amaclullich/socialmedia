CARE THAT WORKS
Prolonged grief disorder
Grief that stays intense and disabling has a name
ILLUSTRATION
Roughly 1 in 10 adults bereaved by a non-violent death
Studies with older participants on average found higher rates

In one US trial of people aged 50 and over, a grief-focused therapy helped about 7 in 10, compared with about 3 in 10 with another talking therapy.

Alasdair MacLullich
Lundorff et al., 2017; Shear et al., JAMA Psychiatry 2014
