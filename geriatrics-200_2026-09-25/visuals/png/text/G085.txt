TEACHING ROUND
Illustrative phrases
Same task, two ways to say it
Elderspeak
Are we ready for our wash, sweetheart?
Good girl.
We'll just pop this on, shall we?
Adult talk
Would you like a wash now or after breakfast?
Thank you.
I'm going to put your cardigan on now. Is that all right?
In 80 filmed care episodes, when a resident's behaviour changed, it was to resistance about 55 times in 100 after elderspeak and about 26 in 100 after adult talk. A link, not proof of cause.
Alasdair MacLullich
Williams et al, 2009; Williams et al, Gerontologist 2017
