ASK THIS
Anything you've stopped doing because you worry about falling?
EXAMPLES
A regular walk
The bus into town
A bath
Visiting a friend

If yes, tell the GP or physiotherapist. Ask about strength and balance exercise, a talking therapy (CBT) or an occupational therapist.

Alasdair MacLullich
World Falls Guidelines 2022; Deshpande et al., J Am Geriatr Soc 2008
