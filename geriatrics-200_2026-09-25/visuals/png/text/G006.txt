'Half of people who lie on the floor for more than an hour die within six months'

Half of 20 people
1981: 125 people aged 65 and over who fell at home, from six general practices
20 lay on the floor for more than an hour
The 20 people who lay for more than an hour
Half of those 20 died within 6 months
All fallers: a quarter died within a year, 5 times as many as a matched comparison group

A small observational series. It shows a link, not a cause.

Alasdair MacLullich
Wild, Nayak and Isaacs, BMJ 1981

What newer studies add
1993
US, 1103 people aged 72 and over: fallers without serious injury who couldn't get up had a lasting loss of everyday abilities more often (35 against 26 in 100). The difference in deaths could have been chance.
2008
Cambridge, people over 90: an hour or more on the floor linked with serious injury; 12 of 20 were admitted after a fall. Deaths not reported.
2022
World Falls Guidelines: an hour or more on the floor marks high risk. Do a full falls assessment.

Newer cohorts link long lies with serious harm. None updates the six-month death figure.

Alasdair MacLullich
Tinetti et al., JAMA 1993; Fleming and Brayne, BMJ 2008; Montero-Odasso et al., Age Ageing 2022
