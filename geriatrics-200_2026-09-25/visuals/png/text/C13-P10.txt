CARE WORTH TRYING
When the toothbrush is pushed away

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE Not a real person. Techniques from a US trial of 101 nursing home residents.

1. The brush is pushed away.
2. The carer comes to eye level, calmly.
3. The person holds a brush at the mirror.
4. The carer starts, then the person carries on.
Alasdair MacLullich
Jablonski et al., Gerodontology 2018. Refusals continued; mouth health showed no clear difference over 3 weeks.
