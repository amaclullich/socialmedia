BEFORE SURGERY
FIRST EYE DONE
Gap between operations: the two eyes can need very different glasses
SECOND EYE DONE
About 1 in 3 (32 in 100) of 179 people waiting for a first eye in one Belfast list were estimated to be at risk
Trial of 239 women over 70: second eye within about 1 month, not 12 months, improved visual function and confidence. Effect on falls uncertain.
Ask before surgery: What should I do about my glasses until the second eye is done?
Alasdair MacLullich
Stuart et al., Ulster Med J 2022; Foss et al., Age and Ageing 2006
