TEACHING ROUND
2/2
A judgement, not a tested remedy
Our suggestion: three habits when estimating survival
Give a range, not a single figure
Say what would shorten it
Ask a colleague's view when you know the patient well

In this study, estimates tended to be less accurate the longer the doctor had known the patient (an association in one study).

Alasdair MacLullich
Christakis and Lamont, BMJ 2000; White et al., PLoS One 2016
