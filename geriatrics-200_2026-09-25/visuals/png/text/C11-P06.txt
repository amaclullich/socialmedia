TEACHING ROUND
Restless legs: check iron and
medicines first

2025 US guideline (AASM). Iron cut-offs are expert consensus, not tested directly.

STEP 1
Check ferritin and transferrin saturation
STEP 2
Offer iron if ferritin is 75 or below, or transferrin saturation is under 20%
STEP 3
Look for aggravators: alcohol, caffeine, sedating antihistamines, serotonergic antidepressants, dopamine blockers, untreated sleep apnoea
STEP 4
Choose treatment with care
Why the guideline advises against dopamine agonists as standard treatment: augmentation (conditional recommendation)
Gabapentinoids in older people: sleepiness and dizziness, weigh after a fall
Alasdair MacLullich
Winkelman et al., J Clin Sleep Med 2025; AGS Beers Criteria 2023
