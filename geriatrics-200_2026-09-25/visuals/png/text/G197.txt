CARE THAT WORKS
Older adults recovered more often after NHS talking therapy in London

Out of every 100 people who attended at least two sessions for depression or anxiety in eight London services, 2008 to 2019. Dropout was recorded by the service after 3 or more sessions.

Aged 65 or over
53

Aged 65 or over: about 53 in 100 recovered. 18 in 100 dropped out.

Aged 18 to 64

Aged 18 to 64: about 41 in 100 recovered. 32 in 100 dropped out.
