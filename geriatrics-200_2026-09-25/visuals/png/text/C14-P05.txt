ASK THIS
Age-related hearing loss
Heard, but not understood

High-pitched sounds are usually affected first, and many consonants are high-pitched.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

Said: sun, fun, thin, fin

sun
fun
thin
fin

May be heard: _un, _un, _in, _in

sun
fun
thin
fin
Suggestions, not trial findings
Get their attention first
Face them
Speak clearly
If they miss it, say it in different words
Alasdair MacLullich
Aydelott et al., Trends in Amplification 2010
