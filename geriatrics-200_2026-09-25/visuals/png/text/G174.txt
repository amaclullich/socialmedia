Two trials, two different results
UK TRIAL
UK: START, 260 carers, 8 coping sessions
Carers' anxiety and depression: lower over 6 years of follow-up (modest difference)
Time to care home move: no clear difference (wide range of results)
US TRIAL
US: 406 spouse carers of people with Alzheimer disease, counselling and support
Time to nursing home move: later, model estimate of the difference in median time about 18 months (557 days)

Different people, eras and designs. Not a head-to-head comparison.

Alasdair MacLullich
Livingston et al., Br J Psychiatry, 2020; Mittelman et al., Neurology, 2006
