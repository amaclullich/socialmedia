TEACHING ROUND
Awake at 2am? Ask before you prescribe

In trials of sleeping tablets taken for at least 5 nights, people over 60 reported about 25 more minutes of sleep on average, with more confusion and daytime tiredness.

Tonight: ask about
Pain
Needing the toilet
Noise from other patients
Medical equipment
Tomorrow, for the day team: review dose times
Steroid given later than the morning?
Donepezil given at night, with sleeplessness or vivid dreams?
Alasdair MacLullich
Wesselius, JAMA Intern Med 2018; Glass, BMJ 2005
