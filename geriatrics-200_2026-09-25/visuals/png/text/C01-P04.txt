CARE THAT WORKS
Four questions before you join a falls prevention class
1.
Will I do balance exercises standing up?
2.
Will it get harder as I improve?
3.
Does it run 3 or more times a week?
4.
Will it last at least 12 weeks, and can I keep going?

Seated classes build strength and can be a start. They have not been shown to prevent falls. If there is no suitable class, ask about a home programme.

Alasdair MacLullich
Sherrington et al., Cochrane 2019; World Falls Guidelines 2022; Klempel et al. 2021
