SMALL DETAIL, REAL EFFECT
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
The first half hour after the weekly bone tablet
First thing, before breakfast. Tablet with a full glass of plain water.
Sit or stand. No lying down.
No coffee, juice, food or other tablets for 30 minutes. In volunteers, coffee or juice cut absorption by about 60%. Coffee waits.
After 30 minutes: breakfast and other medicines.
Alasdair MacLullich
NOGG 2022; Gertz et al., Clin Pharmacol Ther 1995
