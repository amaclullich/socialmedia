Rule: age × 10 µg/L for people aged 50 or over, used after a clinical probability score

Whole study (aged 50 or over, clinical score not high): of 331 people with a D-dimer between 500 µg/L and the age-based limit, not scanned, 1 had a clot within 3 months (0.3%, 95% CI 0.1% to 1.7%)

No extra missed clots found in this group. Management study, no randomised comparison.
