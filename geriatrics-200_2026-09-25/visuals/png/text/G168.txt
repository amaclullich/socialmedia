ASK THIS
Illustration. US study of dementia carers, 1996 to 2000
When your partner moves into a care home, ask:

Carers were as depressed and anxious after the move as before. Husbands and wives were most affected.

Who will check how I am managing?
How does the fees assessment treat our home and my income if I stay in the house?
Can any of the pension stay with me?
Alasdair MacLullich
Schulz et al., JAMA, 2004
