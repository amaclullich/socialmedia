WHO GETS LEFT OUT
Median among carers of people who died of cancer in England, 2015
Carers reported about 69 and a half hours of care a week
Median reported care: 69 h 30 min
One week: 168 hours (for scale only; 69 h 30 min is a median across carers, not one person's week)

Over 9 in 10 carers reported giving care

Self-reported by 1,504 bereaved relatives who had registered a cancer death (28.5% replied). The published abstract does not say whether the hours include time on call. Cancer deaths only.

Alasdair MacLullich
Rowland et al., Palliat Med 2017
