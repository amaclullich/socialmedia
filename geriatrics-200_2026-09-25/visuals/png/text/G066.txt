Contact days in the first year after joining a cancer trial
Nearly 1 day in 5

In six US trials in advanced prostate, lung and pancreatic cancer, people aged 65+ typically spent nearly 1 day in 5 in contact with health care in the year after joining

Ask: how many days will this treatment take, and what might it give back?
Alasdair MacLullich
