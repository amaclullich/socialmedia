Reading the evidence
Blood pressure after 75: check before you aim for a target

SPRINT, the trial behind US and European targets, showed benefit in over-75s well enough to join: about 2.6 vs 3.9 cardiovascular events in 100 a year.

Was this person in the trial population? Not enrolled: care home residents, dementia, diabetes, previous stroke, life expectancy under 3 years
Standing reading: systolic below 110 after 1 minute was an exclusion
A fall of more than 20 on standing: judge treatment on the standing reading (UK guidance)
Falls: previous injurious falls?
Which reading? Clinic readings ran about 5 to 7 mmHg above trial readings
What does the person want from treatment?
