WEIGHING IT UP
Near death, morphine doses often rise

Symptoms change in the last days. Studies in hospices and palliative care units have not found a consistent link between doses adjusted to symptoms and shorter life. One large study found a small link that dose explained very little of.

Evidence: observational studies, mostly in people with cancer
Worried? Ask the nurse or doctor how the dose is matched to the symptoms.
Alasdair MacLullich
Sykes and Thorns, Lancet Oncol 2003; Boland et al., Pain 2015; Portenoy et al., J Pain Symptom Manage 2006
