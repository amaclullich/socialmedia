WHO GETS LEFT OUT
When a client dies, the care worker has lost someone too
Illustration, not a real case
In a New York study, researchers concluded that home care workers' grief when a client died was underestimated by employers and society, particularly the loss of work.
In England, workers said a client's death could leave them drained and under-prepared.
I'm sorry. I know how much you cared about him.
What helped: experienced colleagues, and managers acknowledging their feelings.
You are welcome to come.
For some, time off or encouragement to attend the funeral.
Alasdair MacLullich
Tsui et al., Qual Health Res, 2019; Yeh et al., Int J Geriatr Psychiatry, 2019
