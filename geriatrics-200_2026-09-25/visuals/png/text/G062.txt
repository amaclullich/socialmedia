CARE THAT WORKS
Illustrative example of a written plan
A DNACPR decision is about CPR

DNACPR means 'do not attempt cardiopulmonary resuscitation'. UK guidance: every other treatment and all care is still offered on its merits.

NOT RECOMMENDED
CPR
STILL OFFERED ON ITS MERITS
Regular checks
Calls for senior help if unwell
Pain and symptom relief
Food and drink
Other treatments that may help
Rehabilitation
Company and time with family
Ask: what will still be done?
Alasdair MacLullich
