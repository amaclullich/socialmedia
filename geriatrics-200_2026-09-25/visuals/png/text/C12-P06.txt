CARE THAT WORKS
3/4
340 women, 21 NHS centres, 18 months
No clear difference in quality of life; fewer self-reported complications at 18 months

Women who looked after their own pessary also cost the NHS less. About half answered again at 4 years: still no clear difference in quality of life.

Vaginal tissue concerns on examination (ulceration, granulation or other): about 18 in 100 who self-managed, about 27 in 100 in clinic care. No test reported.

--- card 4 ---
CARE THAT WORKS
4/4
Limits to keep in mind

Not in the trial: women who could not use their hands well enough, women whose memory or thinking problems meant they could not consent or self-manage, women who needed teaching in a language other than English

About 1 in 5 women taught to self-manage went back to clinic care. Clinic care remains a good choice.
