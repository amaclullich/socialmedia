Bowel leakage: how to raise it
ILLUSTRATION, NOT A REAL CONVERSATION

In a US national survey, about 15 in 100 adults aged 70 and over living at home reported leakage in the past month. In two other US surveys (clinic patients in the 1990s; women aged 45 and over), only about 1 in 3 people with leakage had ever talked to a doctor.

I have had some accidents with my bowels.
Say it in plain words. Clinicians are advised to ask directly and to take account of the words a person prefers, such as 'accidents'.
Alasdair MacLullich
Whitehead, Gastroenterology, 2009; Johanson, Am J Gastroenterol, 1996; Assmann, United European Gastroenterol J, 2022

Could a continence service help?
We can start with some simple steps.
First steps help some people: food, fibre and drinks, a toilet routine, pelvic floor exercises, pads and skin care. If these do not help enough, there are further tests and treatments.
