WHAT GETS COUNTED
2/2
Pads or a catheter were linked with new leaking

Using pads or a catheter for most of the stay was linked with new incontinence at discharge, compared with using the toilet, after allowing for age, illness severity, mobility and memory.

This shows a link. It does not show that pads or a catheter caused the leaking.
Frailer people are more likely to be given pads (Danish study of about 2,000 older patients).
Our judgement: help people to the toilet by default, and ask why when a new 'incontinent' record appears.
Alasdair MacLullich
Zisberg et al., J Am Geriatr Soc, 2011; Larsen et al., Int J Nurs Stud Adv, 2023
