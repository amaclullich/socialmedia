WEIGHING IT UP
Melatonin: what to expect and what to ask
What prescribed melatonin is for

Slow-release, ages 55 and over, up to 3 months (European guideline)

What the evidence shows
Alzheimer's disease: may make little or no difference to sleep
Other sleep problems (adults and children together): about 7 minutes quicker to sleep, about 8 minutes more sleep, on average
