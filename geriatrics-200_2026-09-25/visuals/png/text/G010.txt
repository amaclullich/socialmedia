ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
Repeated falls in the first 3 years: think PSP

Falls without a clear cause early in a Parkinson-like illness. Check up-and-down eye movements. Question a Parkinson's diagnosis.

Early falls recorded in past case notes of people whose diagnosis was confirmed at autopsy: about 4 in 100 of 124 with Parkinson's disease, about 1 in 3 of 28 with PSP

Alasdair MacLullich
Höglinger et al., Mov Disord 2017; Fox et al., NPJ Parkinsons Dis 2025

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
Short, wide steps, feet barely lifted: think NPH

More unsteady on turning. Bladder urgency and slowed thinking are common, but many people don't have all three. Selected people often improve after a shunt.

Alasdair MacLullich
Nakajima et al., Neurol Med Chir 2021

3/4
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
Much worse with eyes closed: think position sense or inner ear

Think loss of position sense (for example neuropathy; check B12) or inner-ear balance loss. Read the test with the rest of the examination.

Alasdair MacLullich
Anagnostou et al. 2024 and 2025; Shipton and Thachil, Clin Med 2015
