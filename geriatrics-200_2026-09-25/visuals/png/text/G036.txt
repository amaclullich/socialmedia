READING THE EVIDENCE
Denosumab with low kidney function: plan the calcium

Women 65+ on dialysis, 12 weeks: severe low calcium 41 in 100 v 2 in 100 on a bisphosphonate (observational)

Correct low calcium and vitamin D before the first dose
Check calcium before each injection
CrCl below 35 ml/min: recheck within 2 weeks of the first dose
Tell the person to report symptoms of low calcium
On dialysis: involve the kidney team
Alasdair MacLullich
Bird et al., JAMA 2024; NOGG 2022
