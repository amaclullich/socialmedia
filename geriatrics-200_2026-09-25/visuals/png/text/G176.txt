CARE THAT WORKS
The family carer needs a handover of their own

Illustration, not a real case. Discharge plans that included carers: about a quarter fewer readmissions over 3 to 6 months (relative; multi-part programmes)

Ask what the carer is willing and able to do
Give a written plan
Show the task, then watch the carer show it back
Link the carer to community services
Say who to call with questions

Steps 2 to 4 echo components used in some of the tested programmes. Steps 1 and 5 are our suggestions.
