TEACHING ROUND
3/3
Older person with dizziness
Before you reach for a tablet
Ask what sets the dizziness off
Examine for BPPV with the Dix-Hallpike test
Treat, or refer for treatment, with a repositioning manoeuvre
Avoid routine antihistamines or benzodiazepines
Check mobility, balance and falls risk
If neck extension is hard, a side-lying test was used in a study of 75-year-olds. Its accuracy against Dix-Hallpike is not established.
Alasdair MacLullich
AAO-HNS guideline 2017; Kollen et al. 2012
