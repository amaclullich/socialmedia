WEIGHING IT UP
For a very small group with short life expectancy
Two paths after a broken hip, for a very small group
OPERATION

Surgery, then pain relief, getting up and rehabilitation

CARE WITHOUT AN OPERATION

Should include: regular strong pain relief, careful nursing, support for the family, a written palliative care plan

NICE's guideline group describes surgery as the best form of pain relief. FRAIL-HIP: 172 very frail care home residents who chose, not randomised.

Alasdair MacLullich
Loggers et al, JAMA Surg 2022; NICE CG124
