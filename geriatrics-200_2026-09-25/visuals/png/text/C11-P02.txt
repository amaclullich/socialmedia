TEACHING ROUND
Acting out dreams in later life: four steps

A report is a reason to assess, not a diagnosis.

1
Ask
the bed partner as well as the person. Dreams of chasing or attacking? Injuries?
2
Assess
refer for a sleep study if in doubt. Severe sleep apnoea can look similar.
3
Protect
clear hazards, pad sharp furniture and the headboard, soft rug by the bed.
4
Talk
review medicines, and discuss what it may mean, with time and support.
Alasdair MacLullich
Postuma, Brain 2019; McKeith, Neurology 2017; Howell, JCSM 2023
