READING THE EVIDENCE
One small hospice study of 5 people
Can people hear near the end of life?
ILLUSTRATION
What it found: in 5 people who had stopped responding, all 5 showed a brain response to a change in tones.
What it did not show: that they understood what they heard.
Alasdair MacLullich
Blundon et al., Scientific Reports 2020
