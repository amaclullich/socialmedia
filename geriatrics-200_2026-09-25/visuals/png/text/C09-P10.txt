TEACHING ROUND
Capacity to decide where to live, England and Wales
Before asking 'Can you manage at home?'
A judge: "The person under evaluation must be presented with detailed options so that their capacity to weigh up those options can be fairly assessed."
CC v KK, 2012, paragraph 68
Residence-capacity specialists recommend saying:
1 Say why a move is being suggested.
2 Describe the other options, for example home with carers several times a day.
3 Say what each choice could mean: a care home may become permanent; home with help has no guarantee.
Alasdair MacLullich
Hughes et al, BJPsych Adv 2015; Ruck Keene et al 2019
