SMALL DETAIL, WORTH CHECKING
Does food still smell of anything?

About 6 in 10 people aged 80 and over had a weakened sense of smell when tested. Most did not know.

Pepper
Sauce
Lemon
Herbs

Try: stronger flavours, a sauce, herbs, a squeeze of lemon

Herbs, sauces and lemon are untested. Two trials of commercial flavour enhancers gave mixed results

Ask your GP or pharmacist if a medicine started recently could change taste

Do not stop a medicine on your own

Alasdair MacLullich
