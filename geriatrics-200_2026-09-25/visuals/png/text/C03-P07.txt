ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
The X-ray was normal. She still can't stand on that leg.
The hip X-ray after her fall showed no fracture.
Next morning, with pain relief, she still can't take weight on the leg.
Her care worker asks the question. Could there be a fracture the X-ray didn't show? Does she need an MRI?

Illustrative example, not a real person

NICE: if a fracture is still suspected after normal X-rays, offer MRI, or consider CT if MRI isn't available within 24 hours.
