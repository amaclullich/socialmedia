READING THE EVIDENCE
In one US study, people with more positive views of ageing lived 7.5 years longer

A figure worth checking before you repeat it

1. One study: 660 people aged 50 and over, Ohio

2. A link, not a cause: observational, adjusted for age, sex, socioeconomic status, loneliness and functional health

3. 19 studies pooled: a small link, weaker with longer follow-up

Alasdair MacLullich
Levy et al., J Pers Soc Psychol 2002; Westerhof et al., Psychol Aging 2014
