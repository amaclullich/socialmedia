CARE THAT WORKS
3/3
Ask the cancer team if an assessment is possible

A second US trial (605 people aged 65+, one centre; everyone had an assessment, only one group had a specialist team act on it): serious side effects 51 vs 61 in 100; no clear difference in admissions or survival. US guidance recommends assessment for everyone over 65 with cancer. UK specialists report few patients can get it.

Alasdair MacLullich
Li et al., JAMA Oncol 2021; Dale et al., JCO 2023; Gomes et al., J Geriatr Oncol 2024
