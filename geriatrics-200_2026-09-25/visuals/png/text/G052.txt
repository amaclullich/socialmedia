Three checks for a strong pain patch
1
2
3
1. Old patch off before the new one goes on. Note where and when.
2. No heat over the patch: hot water bottles, heat pads, electric blankets.
3. Fold used patches sticky sides together; dispose of as the pharmacist advises.
A used fentanyl patch still holds enough medicine to harm a child or a pet.
Unusual sleepiness or slow, shallow breathing: get urgent help.
Alasdair MacLullich
Lövborg et al. 2014; Ashburn et al. 2003; Marquardt et al. 1995; Hilado et al. 2020
