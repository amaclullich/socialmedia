A meal shared at the table

1. Food in dishes on the table

2. Residents serve themselves

3. A member of staff or volunteer sits and chats

4. Cloth, glasses, proper plates

5. No interruptions during the meal

Over 6 months: weight held steady with family style meals, against about 1 kg lost with plated meals. Quality of life held steady with family style meals and fell with plated meals. Residents did not have dementia.

Alasdair MacLullich
Nijs et al., BMJ 2006. 178 residents, 5 Dutch nursing homes
