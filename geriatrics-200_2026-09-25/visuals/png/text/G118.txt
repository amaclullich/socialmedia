Smell and colour: weak guides to urine infection

In a small US study, judging by the smell of wet pads disagreed with urine tests about 1 time in 3.

WEAK GUIDES ON THEIR OWN
Smelly urine
A change in urine colour
REPORT PROMPTLY
Pain or burning passing urine
New or much worse urgency, frequency or leaking
Pain low in the tummy or in the side
Blood in the urine
Fever, with any of these
Ask: is the antibiotic for new symptoms, or for smell or colour alone?

People with a catheter, or who are unable to say how they feel, need a clinician's judgement.

Alasdair MacLullich
Midthun, J Gerontol Nurs, 2004; Juthani-Mehta, J Am Geriatr Soc, 2009; Stone, Infect Control Hosp Epidemiol, 2012
