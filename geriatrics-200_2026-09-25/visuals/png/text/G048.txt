READING THE EVIDENCE
Same 5,377 people aged 60+ in England, 35 frailty tools
Which tool decided who was frail?

Share called frail, among the 29 tools with a published cut-off: from about 1 in 100 to about 7 in 10. Most pairs of tools agreed only weakly to moderately on who was frail.

Lowest
about 1 in 100
Highest

Questions to ask
Which tool was used?
Which cut-off?
Were the people studied identified the same way as the person in front of you?
