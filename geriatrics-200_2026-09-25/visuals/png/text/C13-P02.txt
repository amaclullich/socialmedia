READING THE EVIDENCE
Mouth care and pneumonia: the evidence is uncertain
WHAT TRIALS SHOW ABOUT PNEUMONIA
14 US nursing homes, 2,152 residents: no clear fall in pneumonia over 2 years
Cochrane 2022, 6 trials, all high risk of bias: effect largely unclear
Two small trials: fewer pneumonia deaths at 2 years (low certainty); a larger study: no clear difference at 18 months
No trial compared mouth care with none
REASONS TO CLEAN MOUTHS ANYWAY (JUDGEMENT)
Removes plaque, the main cause of tooth decay and gum disease
Comfort
Easier eating
Sore spots and broken teeth found early
Dignity
