ILLUSTRATIVE FLOW, NOT TRIAL DATA
In trials, community vision screening did not improve older people's vision

We should judge screening by whether sight gets corrected.

Where screening can fail: the later steps
UK trial: 4,340 people aged 75 and over in 20 practices. Testing everyone did not leave people seeing better at 3 to 5 years than testing some.
Cochrane review: 10 trials, 10,608 people living independently. Reviewers' view: most likely reason was low take-up of the treatment offered.
Alasdair MacLullich
Smeeth et al., BMJ 2003; Clarke et al., Cochrane 2018
