ASK THIS
A fall weeks ago can explain new symptoms now

Chronic subdural haematoma: a slow bleed between the brain and the skull

Slow bleed
Skull
Brain
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
DAY OF THE FALL

The fall or knock to the head. It may seem minor, or be forgotten.

DAYS TO WEEKS LATER

New unsteadiness or falls, headache, weakness on one side, slower or muddled thinking, drowsiness.

WHAT TO DO

Tell the doctor about the fall. Ask whether a brain scan is needed.

Often treatable. Sudden or severe symptoms: get emergency help.
