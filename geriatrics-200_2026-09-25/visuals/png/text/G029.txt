If your relative can't decide for themselves
In England and Wales, next of kin can't consent to surgery for an adult who can't decide
England and Wales: a registered health and welfare attorney or a court-appointed deputy decides. If there is neither, the doctor responsible decides in the person's best interests. Family or close friends are consulted if there is time.
Scotland: a doctor completes a certificate of incapacity. If a welfare attorney has been appointed, their consent is sought where reasonable and practicable.

In both nations, the person's wishes must be taken into account.

Alasdair MacLullich
Wade and Kitzinger 2019; Orr and Baruah 2018; Edwardson and Baruah 2018

Three questions to ask

1. Who is making this decision, and on what basis?

2. Could they decide for themselves, with help or at another time?

3. How will what they would want be taken into account?
