READING THE EVIDENCE
1/2
One conversation, adults generally
What 18 trials of teach-back showed
(knowledge: 9 trials; confidence: 5; adherence: 7)
Knowledge: no clear pattern. Certainty: very low.
Confidence in managing health: increased. Certainty: very low.
Sticking to advice, self-reported, short term: increased. Certainty: low.

Certainty is shown in words and by pattern.

Alasdair MacLullich
Vick et al, J Gen Intern Med 2026
