ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
Ask for the letter to be written to you
Written about you

Dear Dr Patel, I reviewed this 81-year-old woman in clinic. She reports dizziness on standing.

Written to you

Dear Mrs Grant,

Today we talked about your dizziness.

What we found.

What happens next.

Who to call.

Could you write the letter to me, please?

National guidance recommends clinic letters are written to the patient.

At one London eye hospital, about 2 in 100 of 4.6 million letters (2013 to 2025) were judged to be written to the patient.
Alasdair MacLullich
Academy of Medical Royal Colleges 2018; Ong et al, BMJ Health Care Inform 2026
