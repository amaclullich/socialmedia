Your knee X-ray is a poor guide to your pain

Many people with arthritis changes on the X-ray have little pain. Many with knee pain have fairly normal X-rays.

Alasdair MacLullich
Bedson and Croft, BMC Musculoskelet Disord 2008

--- card 2 ---
WHAT GETS COUNTED
2/3
It still tells you something

Within one person, the worse knee on X-ray is more often the painful one.

Typical symptoms: an X-ray is not needed to diagnose knee osteoarthritis

Something unusual or another possible cause: imaging helps. Unexpected worsening: consider imaging

Alasdair MacLullich
Neogi et al., BMJ 2009; EULAR 2017
