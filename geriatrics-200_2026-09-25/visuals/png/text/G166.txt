WHAT GETS COUNTED
Scottish GP records, 2013 to 2016
Before discharging for non-attendance, ask:

People over 90 and people in the most deprived areas were among the most likely to miss appointments repeatedly.

Did the letter arrive, and could they read it?
Could they hear the phone?
How would they get there?
Who would bring them?
Could they afford the fare?
Did they remember the date?

Practical suggestion, not a tested protocol

Alasdair MacLullich
Ellis et al., Lancet Public Health, 2017; McQueenie et al., BMC Medicine, 2019
