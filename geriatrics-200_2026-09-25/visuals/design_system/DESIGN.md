# Visual design guide: geriatrics 200 collection

Every post has a finished visual built in HTML and SVG, rendered to PNG by `render.js`. The HTML file is the editable source. Nothing is generated by an image model, so every word and every number on a visual is exactly what the source file says.

## Files and naming

- Source: `visuals/src/<ID>.html`, one file per post, containing one `.card` per image (a carousel has 2 to 4 cards).
- Render: from `visuals/design_system/`, run `node render.js ../src/<ID>.html`. Output: `visuals/png/<ID>.png` (single) or `<ID>_1.png` ... (carousel), plus `visuals/png/text/<ID>.txt` with the visible text of each card for read-back.
- The report lists layout problems: anything outside the card, clipped text, text under 22 px, SVG text clipped by its SVG, content overflow, em or en dashes, exclamation marks, spaced hyphens, fonts not loaded, and oversize files. A visual is not finished until the report for it is clean.
- Link the design system with relative paths from `visuals/src/`: `<link rel="stylesheet" href="../design_system/base.css">`, `<script src="../design_system/chart.js"></script>`, `<script src="../design_system/kit.js"></script>`.
- If a page runs scripts that draw, set `window.__ready = false` at the top and `window.__ready = true` after drawing, so the renderer waits.

## The card

1080 by 1350 layout px (4:5), rendered at 2x. Structure:

```html
<div class="card" id="s1">                      <!-- add "dark", "mint" or "sand" for other grounds -->
  <div class="topline"><span class="series">Weighing it up</span><span class="slidecount">1/3</span></div>
  <div class="content"> ... </div>              <!-- add "center" to centre vertically -->
  <div class="foot"><span class="author">Alasdair MacLullich</span><span class="src">Source: Author et al., Journal Year</span></div>
</div>
```

- Series label: always the post's series name. Slide count only on carousels.
- Footer: always "Alasdair MacLullich"; the right side carries the short source credit on evidence visuals, or is left empty.
- Grounds: light (default, off-white) for most; `dark` (deep teal) for covers, single big numbers and closing slides; `mint` and `sand` for variety. Gold text only on dark. Burnt orange only on light grounds.

## Type

- Headline: `h1` (84 px), `h1.xl` (100), `h1.m` (70), `h1.s` (60). Use `<em>` inside a headline for the one phrase that carries the point (orange on light, gold on dark).
- `.kicker` (30 px, bold, orange) above a headline for a short label.
- `.lead` (40 px) for the one sentence that explains the headline; `.body` (34 px) for supporting text; `.small` (28 px) for notes and definitions. Never below 24 px except the footer source line.
- Restraint: a single card should rarely carry more than about 45 words. Phone-size test: view the PNG at 540 px wide; every word must still be readable.

## Components (all in base.css)

- Big number: `.bignum` (240 px) or `.bignum.m` (180 px) with `.bignum-label` beneath. A big number always has its meaning in the label: denominator, population, time period.
- Numbered steps: `<ol class="steps"><li><b>Short heading</b>Text</li></ol>`.
- Checklist or questions: `<ul class="ticks"><li>...</li><li class="q">...</li></ul>` (`.q` shows a question mark marker).
- Two-column comparison: `.compare > .col.a` and `.col.b`, each with `.tag`, `h3` and a `ul`. Column A has hollow round markers on white, column B solid square markers on mint, so the difference is not carried by colour alone. Only compare things that are genuinely comparable.
- Stacked comparison rows: `.rowcompare > .row > .k + .v`.
- Flow: `.flow` with `.step` (add `.alt` or `.fill`) and `.arrow` between steps (text inside `.arrow` labels the transition).
- Timeline: `.timeline > .ev > .t + .d` (`.ev.hot` for the highlighted event).
- Panels: `.panel` (white), `.panel.mint`, `.panel.deep`, `.panel.sand`.
- Badges: `.badge`, `.badge.illustrative` (dashed, for "Illustrative example"), `.badge.measured` (for measured results next to an illustrative panel).
- Comic strip: `.strip.p3` (three stacked panels) or `.strip.p4` (two by two), each `.panelbox` with `.art` (an SVG drawn with the kit) and `.cap` (caption text). Dialogue, if any, goes in the caption as plain text, or in a `.bubble` positioned over the art. Every comic carries a `.badge.illustrative` saying "Illustrative example" unless the caption makes that obvious.
- Annotated scene: `.scene` containing an SVG with class `art`, numbered `.pin` markers positioned with inline `left`/`top` percentages, and a numbered key below the scene or `.callout` boxes. Keep callouts off faces and hands.

## Charts (chart.js)

- `barChart(target, data, opts)`: horizontal bars from a zero baseline. `data = [{label, value, display, colour: 'a'|'b'|'empty'}]`, `opts = {max, width: 920, ticks: [0, 10, 20], tickFmt}`.
- `iconArray(target, {filled, total: 100, cols: 10, size, gap, shape: 'square'|'person', fillColour: 'a'|'b'})`: 10 by 10 arrays for "N in 100". Put the count and "in 100" in text under each array. For two groups use two arrays side by side with the comparator first.
- `stackBar(target, segs, opts)`: one 100% bar with labelled segments.
- Series colours: A `#00909A` (teal) and B `#C24E12` (burnt orange), validated for colour-vision separation. Always add a legend or direct labels.
- Rules: only verified numbers from the evidence library; every chart states what is counted, the denominator or population, and the time period; no dual axes; no numbers read off a published figure by eye; absolute values before relative ones; no confidence intervals on public-facing cards; practitioner cards may show an interval as text.

## Drawn illustrations (kit.js)

See `KIT.md` for the people and props available. Rules:

- Faces are featureless; posture and head angle carry emotion.
- Vary people across the collection: sex, skin tone, build, hair, disability, mobility aids, settings. Show older people active and engaged as well as unwell; not every older person is frail, confused or alone.
- Equipment drawn accurately and used correctly (frames stepped into, sticks held with tip on the floor, wheelchair footplates under feet, call bells within reach when the point is that they are within reach).
- No text of any kind inside the drawing: no signs, screens, labels, posters, calendars or clocks with numbers. All words are HTML, laid out over or beside the art.
- Never draw restraint, distress for effect, nudity, or anyone as a joke.

## Alt text

One per card, 400 characters or fewer. Describe what the card says and shows: the finding, the numbers with their meaning, the comparison, the limitation. For drawings, say briefly who is shown doing what. Do not start with "Image of".

## Read-back check (every card, every time)

1. Open the PNG and look at it at full size and at 540 px wide.
2. Compare `png/text/<ID>.txt` with the approved visual text word for word. Any difference is fixed in the HTML and re-rendered.
3. Check any chart against its data: bar lengths, counts of filled marks, labels, denominators, time periods.
4. Check the drawing against the brief: the right people, posture, equipment and setting; nothing broken (limbs, joints, floating figures); nothing that stereotypes or mocks.
5. Check nothing touches the card edge, nothing overlaps, and the render report is clean.
